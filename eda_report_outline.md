# EDA Report Outline

1. Define business context, objectives, and research questions.
2. Document data sources, coverage, variables, units, and methodology.
3. Profile data quality, including missing values, duplicates, and inconsistent fields.
4. Summarize distributions and descriptive statistics.
5. Compare production, yield, price, and arrivals by crop, market, region, and year.
6. Explore time trends and seasonality with line charts.
7. Use bar charts, histograms, box plots, scatter plots, and correlation heatmaps where appropriate.
8. Investigate statistical anomalies and document whether they are errors, valid events, or unresolved.
9. Interpret correlations cautiously and avoid causal claims without suitable evidence.
10. Present verified findings, limitations, recommendations, and future work.

## Expected outcomes
- Dataset profile and quality summary.
- Descriptive tables and visualizations.
- Regional and seasonal patterns for further review.
- A documented anomaly register.
- Evidence-based recommendations and priorities for future analysis.
